Robot bumpers are the padded structures around a competition robot's frame. I developed a configurable two-piece assembly for the Zebracorns so we could adapt our bumper design to a new chassis without starting the CAD from scratch each season.

The Onshape model uses input variables for frame dimensions, frame-to-bumper spacing, split openings, and mounting height. Changing those inputs updates the backing parts and assembly relationships. Sliding tabs and spring pins provide a quick-release mounting approach.

The practical goal was to save design time during build season and get fabrication quotes sooner. I shared the model and its configuration instructions in our [Chief Delphi build thread](https://www.chiefdelphi.com/t/the-zebracorns-behind-the-stripes-design-code-and-build-blog-2023-2024/440094/11?u=theguywhoasked).

This work later inspired SWYFT Robotics' bumper system, which we used in 2025 alongside more than 100 other teams. The original assembly was designed around the 2024 rules; adapting it for another robot or season requires checking mounting clearances and the applicable rules.
